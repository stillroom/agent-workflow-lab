Northside Garden Care (synthetic test business, not a real client).

Owner plus four field staff, Melbourne. Staff mark garden-maintenance jobs
complete in a field-service app. The owner raises invoices from the same system
each afternoon. Most customers pay by bank transfer within seven days. The owner
checks overdue invoices every Friday and personally follows up the small number
that remain unpaid. This process is currently manageable and the owner has not
asked to change it.

The business receives warm recommendations and has many satisfied repeat
customers, but review requests are inconsistent. A staff member sometimes
remembers to send a generic text message after a job, but there is no reliable
trigger, no record of who was contacted, and no suppression for customers who
already reviewed the business. The owner estimates that around three quarters of
completed jobs receive no review request at all.

The owner wants a simple process that prepares a polite review-request message
after a paid job. They want to approve the wording and the eligible customer
rules before the first use. They do not want messages sent automatically during
the trial. They would like to discuss a small pilot this month, but say there is
no emergency and normal work should continue while it is considered.
